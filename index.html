Our Story

From HIV/AIDS Response to Community Development

FORCAS was established in 2005 when HIV and AIDS left many children and families vulnerable. One orphan who received community-based assistance is now an adult and civil service officer.

"When we invest in a child today, we invest in the future of a community."

informed consent   stories shared with permission

Vision: A Malawi where children are protected, young people empowered, and communities resilient.

Mission

Strengthen communities through inclusive and sustainable programmes that protect children, improve health, empower youth, and build resilience.

Values

Integrity · Accountability · Dignity & Respect · Inclusion · Partnership · Community Ownership · Learning & Innovation

Community-rooted

We work through community participation, partnerships, practical solutions, and evidence-based learning since 2005.

Our Programmes

Health & HIV Prevention

HIV prevention, health education, youth engagement, and community participation.

Youth Empowerment & Skills

WASH, Nutrition & Resilience

Safe water, hygiene, nutrition awareness, stronger communities.
